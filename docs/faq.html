Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about KDCN Store services.

How do I order a service?

Reach out to KDCN via WhatsApp or email info@kingmandigital.co.ke. Let us know which service you're interested in and we'll confirm scope, fee, timeline, and payment details before work begins.

Which tier of service should I choose?

Services are grouped into four tiers based on the level of support and digital investment required:

  • Essential — quick tasks, government services, individual assistance
  • Professional — career, branding, and small business foundations
  • Business & Growth — websites and multi-platform growth
  • Advanced — comprehensive digital identity and infrastructure

Not sure? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose the right service.

How much do services cost?

Fees are listed on each service page under "Service Fee." Final pricing may vary depending on scope, complexity, and specific requirements. We always confirm the fee in writing before work begins.

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, and other agreed payment methods. Payment instructions are provided once the scope and fee are confirmed.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, for most services a deposit is required before work begins. The remaining balance is paid upon delivery. Specific terms are confirmed per project.

How long does delivery take?

Timelines vary per service:

  • Essential services — usually same day to 48 hours
  • Professional services — 2–5 working days
  • Website and business services — 1–3 weeks depending on scope

Exact timelines are confirmed before work begins.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Design and branding services include up to 2 revision rounds. Additional revisions or scope changes may attract additional fees. See each service page for details.

What if a service depends on a government platform (e.g. eCitizen, KRA)?

Government and third-party platform services (eCitizen, KRA, SHA, NSSF, Google, etc.) are subject to each platform's availability, verification requirements, and processing times. We assist you with the process but cannot override those requirements.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow our Terms of Service:

  • Before work begins — full refund of deposit
  • During work — partial refund based on completed work
  • After delivery — no refunds on completed services
  • Third-party fees (domain, hosting) — non-refundable once paid
Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. KDCN serves clients across Africa and internationally. Note that services dependent on Kenyan government platforms (eCitizen, KRA, etc.) require Kenyan identification and are only available to clients with the necessary access.

Is my information safe?

Yes. We treat all client information as confidential and follow the principle: "No data left unguarded. No system left unsafe." We do not share client data with third parties except where required to deliver the service or by law.

Who owns the work after delivery?

Upon full payment, you receive rights to use the deliverables produced for you. KDCN retains the right to reference completed work in portfolios unless otherwise agreed. Third-party assets (fonts, images, plugins) remain subject to their own licences.

Can I speak to someone before ordering?

Absolutely. Reach us on WhatsApp at +254 111 843 716 or email info@kingmandigital.co.ke. We're happy to discuss your needs before you commit.

Still have a question? Chat with us on WhatsApp.
