Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how KDCN Store (operated by Kingman Digital Cyber Network) uses cookies and similar technologies on shop.kingmandigital.co.ke.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences over time, improving your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic website functionality
  • Functional cookies — remember your preferences (e.g. dark/light mode)
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site (if enabled)

3. Cookies We Use

Below is a list of cookies used on this website:

Cookie Type Purpose
kdcn-shop-theme Functional Remembers your dark/light mode preference
_cfuvid Essential Cloudflare security identifier
cf_clearance Essential Cloudflare bot protection clearance

We do not currently use advertising or marketing cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Your browser settings — most browsers allow you to block, delete, or manage cookies
  • Browser-specific instructions:
    • Chrome
    • Firefox
    • Safari
    • Edge

Blocking cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Cloudflare — security and performance (essential)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting

These providers have their own cookie and privacy policies.

6. Consent

By continuing to use KDCN Store, you consent to the use of essential and functional cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy. The "Last Updated" date reflects any changes.
