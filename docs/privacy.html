Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

KDCN Store ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Kingman Digital Cyber Network (KDCN). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use shop.kingmandigital.co.ke or engage our services.

This policy complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act (POPIA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

"No data left unguarded. No system left unsafe." — This is our engineering mandate and our commitment to you.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Contact details — name, email, phone number, WhatsApp number
  • Order information — service selected, project requirements, documents you provide
  • Payment information — M-Pesa transaction references (we do not store card details)
  • Communication — messages sent to us via WhatsApp, email, or contact forms

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, referring source
  • Device data — browser, operating system, general location
  • Cookies — small files stored on your device (see our Cookie Policy)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the services you've ordered
  • To confirm scope, timelines, and fees in writing
  • To respond to your questions and support requests
  • To process payments and issue receipts
  • To improve our services and website
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:

  • Contract — to deliver services you've ordered
  • Consent — where you've explicitly agreed (e.g. marketing)
  • Legitimate interest — improving our services and website security
  • Legal obligation — tax, regulatory, and compliance requirements

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Trusted service providers — hosting, payment processing, communication tools
  • Legal authorities — where required by law or court order
  • Government platforms — only when you've asked us to assist with a specific government service

5. Data Security

We protect your data through:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Restricted access to personal information
  • Secure communication channels
  • Regular review of our security practices
  • Data minimisation — we only collect what we need

While we take security seriously, no system is 100% secure. We continually improve our protections.

6. Data Retention

  • Order and service records — retained for 5 years (Kenyan tax requirement)
  • Contact inquiries — retained for up to 24 months
  • Website analytics — retained for up to 12 months
  • Cookie data — based on your preferences

7. Your Rights (POPIA & GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete — request deletion of your data (where legally possible)
  • Restrict — request restriction of processing
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent

To exercise any right, contact us at info@kingmandigital.co.ke. We respond within 30 days.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may process data on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — DNS, CDN, security
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Google Analytics — website analytics (if enabled)
  • WhatsApp Business — customer communication

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We choose providers that meet our security standards.

9. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience. See our Cookie Policy for details and controls.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it.

11. International Transfers

Some third-party providers may process data outside Kenya. Where this happens, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last Updated" date reflects any changes. Continued use of the store means you accept the updated policy.
